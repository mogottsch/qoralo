Why ExecuTorch Blocked vs ONNX Success

In experiment 0012, PyTorch's torch.export failed on FlowLM due to data-dependent dynamic slicing guards in its stateful attention cache. ONNX Runtime succeeded by making past KV caches explicit named input/output graph tensors.

iSTFTNet Vocoder & STFT Exact
iSTFT / Harmonic
Combines neural residual upsampling with direct mathematical Inverse Short-Time Fourier Transform.
Tensor Transformation Contract
Port Shape Description
Magnitude |S| [1, N_fft/2 + 1, T_frames] Linear magnitude spectrogram predicted by the generator.
Phase (Real, Imag) [1, N_fft/2 + 1, T_frames, 2] Complex exponential phase estimates.
Waveform Out [1, 1, T_samples] Continuous 24,000 Hz audio waveform.
Exact iSTFT Overlap-Add Formula
s_m(t) = IDFT( |S_m| · e^{j · ∠S_m} ) · w(t) x(t) = ∑_m s_m(t - m·H) / ∑_m w²(t - m·H) Where w(t) is a Hann window and H is the hop size (300 samples).
Why iSTFTNet is Extremely Fast

Traditional HiFi-GAN vocoders require 4-5 stages of transposed convolutions to upsample by 256x-320x sample by sample. iSTFTNet only upsamples feature maps by a fraction (e.g. 4x) and offloads the final high-resolution acoustic reconstruction directly to the mathematical Inverse Fourier Transform.
